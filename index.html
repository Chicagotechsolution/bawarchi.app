How it works

Three steps, no phone tag.

1Browse kitchens near you

Restaurants and licensed home kitchens, each with its dishes, pickup windows, and halal sourcing shown plainly.

2Order and pay in the app

Pick a time that suits the pot — some dishes are made to order, some are today's rotation.

3Pick up hot

Skip the line, grab the bag with your name on it. Delivery comes later; we're starting with pickup done right.

For kitchens

Built for the bawarchi as much as the eater.

If you run a halal restaurant or a home kitchen, Bawarchi is the counter queue, the ringing phone, and the WhatsApp order chaos — replaced by one clean order screen.

  • Your menu, your prices, your pickup windows
  • Mark a pot "finished for today" in one tap
  • Show your halal sourcing on every listing
  • Fair, flat fees — published before you sign up
